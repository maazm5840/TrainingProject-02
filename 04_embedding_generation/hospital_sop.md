# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Hospitals under ESIC / ESIS 2025

## 1. Introduction
The Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) remains steadfast in its mission to deliver equitable, accessible, and quality healthcare services to insured persons and their families under the ESI Scheme.

This SOP provides standardized procedures for hospital administration, patient care, and operational management.

## 2. Objective
- To ensure uniform and quality healthcare delivery across all ESIC/ESIS hospitals
- To establish clear guidelines for medical and non-medical staff
- To improve patient safety and satisfaction

## 3. Scope
This SOP is applicable to all ESIC and ESIS Hospitals across India.

### 3.1 Key Areas Covered
1.  Outpatient Department (OPD) Management
2.  Inpatient Department (IPD) Management
3.  Emergency Services
4.  Pharmacy and Drug Management
5.  Laboratory Services
