ECG MACHINE EQUIPMENT MANUAL

Purpose:
This manual explains the operation and maintenance of the ECG machine.

Operating Instructions:
1. Connect the ECG machine to the power supply.
2. Attach the patient electrodes correctly.
3. Turn on the machine and select the required settings.
4. Record the patient's ECG.
5. Save or print the results.

Maintenance:
Clean the electrodes after use.
Inspect the cables regularly.
Keep the machine in a dry and safe location.
Report any equipment malfunction to the biomedical department.

Safety:
Only trained hospital staff should operate the ECG machine.
